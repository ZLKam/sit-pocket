ONE-TAP ACCESS

Add SIT Pocket to your iPhone

Open this page in Safari, then follow these three steps.

  1. 1 Tap the Share button ⇧ in Safari.
  2. 2 Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add. SIT Pocket will appear like an app.

ONE-TIME SETUP

Connect MediHub to SIT Pocket

MediHub does not provide a working web launch address, so an Apple Shortcut handles the handoff.

  1. 1 Tap Open Shortcuts below to start a new shortcut.
  2. 2 Choose Add Action → Open App, then select Howden MediHub.
  3. 3 Rename it exactly Open MediHub, tap Done, then return here.
Open Shortcuts Need the app? Open the App Store ↗

After setup, the MediHub card launches this shortcut directly. You can reopen these instructions from “MediHub setup” at the bottom of the page.

PRIVATE CALENDAR

Connect your timetable

Sync from in4SIT on your computer, then open the private iPhone setup link created by the extension.

  1. 1 Open your timetable in in4SIT on Chrome and sync it with the SIT Pocket extension.
  2. 2 Select Copy iPhone setup link in the extension.
  3. 3 Send the link to this iPhone and open it. SIT Pocket connects automatically.
